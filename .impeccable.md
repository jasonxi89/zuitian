## Design Context

### Users
Public-facing web app for anyone looking for clever conversation starters, pickup lines, and chat replies. Users are typically in the middle of a conversation (WeChat, dating apps, etc.) and need quick, one-tap access to the right phrase. Speed and scannability are paramount — they're multitasking.

### Brand Personality
**Fun, Premium, Mysterious** — like a well-dressed friend who always knows exactly what to say. Not try-hard or cringe, but effortlessly charming. The interface should feel like opening a secret weapon, not a generic utility app.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Tone**: Sophisticated with playful undertones — think luxury fragrance branding meets a cheeky wink
- **Theme**: Dual mode (light + dark), respecting system preference
- **Typography**: Move away from Inter to something with more personality — a distinctive display font paired with a clean CJK-friendly body font
- **Color**: Replace the generic purple-gold AI palette with something more intentional and ownable. Consider warm, alluring tones (deep wine, champagne gold, warm neutrals) or unexpected combinations that evoke mystery
- **Effects**: Replace glassmorphism and glow orbs with subtler, more purposeful treatments — depth through layering and shadow rather than blur and glow
- **Anti-patterns to fix**: gradient text, generic dark-mode-with-neon, Inter font, decorative glow orbs, glassmorphism overuse

### Design Principles
1. **Instant access** — Users are mid-conversation. Every interaction should be fast, one-tap, zero friction.
2. **Quietly premium** — Quality shows in restraint: careful spacing, considered typography, subtle motion. Not flashy.
3. **Playful mystery** — Spark curiosity and delight without being childish. Surprise through micro-interactions, not decoration.
4. **Universally legible** — Public-facing means diverse users. Strong contrast, clear hierarchy, accessible by default.
5. **Dual personality** — Light mode feels fresh and inviting; dark mode feels intimate and alluring. Both feel like the same brand.
